Program Highlights

What to Expect

A day packed with spirit, sweat, laughter, and love — here's a glimpse of what we have in store for the whole family.

🙏

Morning Opening

Morning Worship & Praise

We begin as one body — lifting hearts and voices in gratitude, setting the spirit for the day ahead.

🏀

Morning Activity

Basketball & Volleyball on the Court + Palarong Pinoy

The youth absolutely love this — full-court energy, friendly competition, and a whole lot of cheering from the sidelines.

🎉

Throughout the Day

Group Activities for All Ages

From the youngest to the eldest — games and activities designed so everyone joins in. Youths, young adults, and elders all play together.

🍽️

Midday

Lunch & Open Fellowship

Sit, eat, laugh, and catch up. Good food is even better when shared with family — and this is exactly that.

🎮

Afternoon

Afternoon Games & Mini-Events

The fun continues with afternoon games, mini competitions, and surprises that will keep everyone on their feet (or their seats).

🏆

Closing

Closing Program — Awards, Sharing & Group Photo

We end together — celebrating our champions, sharing hearts, and freezing the memory in one big, beautiful family photo.

All activities are tentative and will be finalized closer to the date. Stay tuned for updates!
